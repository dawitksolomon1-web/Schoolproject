Why the Signature sells

Full detail plus ceramic bought separately is $618 for a 5-seat SUV. The Signature is $549, a real $69 saving, so it reads as the smart choice. Full detail at $419 is only $130 less, which makes skipping ceramic feel like a bad deal.

Where it sits in the market

Local mobile detailers charge about $150–500 for a detail with no protection. Shop ceramic packages usually start near $500 with no interior work. The Signature sits above the cleaners and below the coating shops, and it's the only one that does both at your house.

Guarantee

"Beads water for 12 months or we reapply free." It applies when the car is hand-washed. The ceramic is cheap to reapply (about $70), and only a few customers will claim, so it costs maybe $5–10 per order. It's the strongest promise in the local market.

How we take the market

1. Outbid on ads

About $268 profit per order means we can pay up to about $170 to win a customer and still make $100. Competitors charging $250 for a detail can't match that bid, so we get the top ad spots.

2. Different category

Don't compete on "car wash price." Every ad sells the result that lasts: "Your car, sealed for a year." Show the water-beading clip at the end of every job; detailers film it as part of their photo checklist.

3. Speed

Same-day and next-day slots from a pool of detailers, with a $50 credit for booking same day. A solo detailer is booked out a week, so we win the "I need it now" customers.

4. Built-in repeat

A month-11 renewal text at 15% off. If 30% come back, each customer adds about $55 more profit on average in year two, with no subscription to sell.

5. Start rich, then expand

Launch in McLean, Great Falls, Vienna, Arlington, Bethesda, Potomac and Ashburn. Add zip codes only after cost per order holds below about $170.

Let the market set the price: a 3-week test

  1. Run identical ads to three copies of the price page: Signature at $499, $549 and $599 (5-seat SUV; other sizes move by the same amount).
  2. Each copy gets the same budget, about $40–60 a day each, for 2–3 weeks or until each has about 25 bookings.
  3. Pick the winner by profit per ad dollar, not by booking rate. The cheapest page will get the most bookings and still often lose.
  4. Then test the next step up. Stop raising the price when profit per ad dollar falls.

Show the same price to everyone in an area during a test, and keep it honest: the page shows the real price, and the "saving" is always measured against real à la carte prices.

Delivery

  • Detailer pay is set high on purpose: $245–300 for a Signature, depending on size. That's above market so we get the best independent detailers and keep them. Higher prices pay for this.
  • Each Signature takes about 4–5 hours, so one detailer does 2 a day. 100 orders a month needs about 3 full-time-equivalent detailers.
  • Photo and video checklist on every job, including the water-beading clip, and payout only after you approve it.
  • A spray-applied SiO₂ ceramic sealant, the same product and method on every job, sold as "12-month ceramic protection," never as a multi-year coating.
  • Say "vetted, insured detailers." Never claim an in-house team.
