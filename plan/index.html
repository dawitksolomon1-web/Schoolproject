Copy

Tilicare's instant price, $50 refundable deposit, pay-after-service, "we bring water and power," and the same/next-day bonus.

Beat

Nobody leads with a ceramic bundle or asks again after payment. AutoDetail DMV's quote-on-arrival pricing is friction, and your fixed prices win against it.

Be careful

Tilicare sells against outsourcing ("never third party"). Never claim your team is in-house. Say "vetted, insured, background-checked detailers" and show the photo-checked guarantee. Use your own wording and design, not theirs.

Launch in five steps

1. Detailers

Hire 3–5 local mobile detailers who have their own rig and insurance. Use a flat rate per size, plus about $60 for each ceramic add-on. Run one paid trial each and require photos on every job.

2. Checkout

Build the 5 screens above in-house. Stripe saves the card with the deposit, so the balance and any one-tap add-on are charged after the job.

3. Texts

Automated texts: confirmation, a day-before reminder with "reply YES to add ceramic," on-the-way, photos, review request, and a 20%-off ceramic follow-up.

4. Ads

Google Search for "mobile detailing near me" in NoVA, DC and MD zip codes. Meta video ads sending people to the price page. Judge each by cost per paid order.

5. Measure

Every week, check cost per order, ceramic attach rate, and average order value. Scale when cost per order is under about $100.
